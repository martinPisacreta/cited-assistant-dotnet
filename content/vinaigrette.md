# Making a simple vinaigrette


Start with three parts oil to one part vinegar by volume. For a small batch, use three tablespoons of olive oil and one tablespoon of vinegar. This is a starting ratio, not a rule: adjust it to your taste and the strength of the vinegar.

Put the vinegar in a bowl with a small pinch of salt. Whisk, then slowly add the oil while continuing to whisk. Alternatively, combine the ingredients in a clean jar, close the lid firmly, and shake.

Taste a little dressing on a salad leaf. Add a little more oil if it tastes too sharp, or a few drops of vinegar if it needs more acidity. Taste again before adding more salt.

Oil and vinegar naturally separate, so whisk or shake again immediately before dressing the salad. This lesson covers a basic dressing for immediate use; it does not establish storage times, preservation methods, or allergy substitutions.
