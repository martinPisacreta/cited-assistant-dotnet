# Roasting vegetables


Heat the oven to 220°C / 425°F. Wash the vegetables, dry them well, and cut them into similar-sized pieces so they cook more evenly.

Toss the pieces with enough oil to coat them lightly. Spread them in a single layer on a rimmed baking tray, leaving space between the pieces. If the tray is crowded, use a second tray: trapped moisture encourages steaming and reduces browning.

Roast until the vegetables are tender and browned at the edges, turning them partway through. Start checking after 20 minutes; the total time depends on the vegetable, piece size, and oven. Use tenderness and browning rather than a fixed timer as your finish cues.

Season to taste before serving. This lesson covers roasting vegetables only; it does not provide instructions for meat, food preservation, or medical dietary needs.
