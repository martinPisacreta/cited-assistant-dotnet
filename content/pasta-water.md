# Finishing pasta with cooking water


Before draining cooked pasta, reserve a mug of its cooking water. The water contains starch released by the pasta and may also contain salt from the cooking pot.

Transfer the drained pasta to a pan with the prepared sauce. Over low heat, add a small splash of the reserved water and toss thoroughly. The starch and movement help the sauce coat the pasta.

Add more water a little at a time if the sauce is too thick. Stop when the sauce coats the pasta without leaving a watery pool. If it becomes too loose, keep tossing over low heat briefly and check again.

Taste before adding more salt because both the sauce and the reserved water may already be seasoned. Serve promptly. This lesson does not specify cooking times for individual pasta shapes or provide recipes for the sauce itself.
